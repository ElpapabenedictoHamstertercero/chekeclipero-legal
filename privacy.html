Privacy Policy – Chekeclipero

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Access and Process

Chekeclipero accesses only the information and permissions explicitly authorized through TikTok OAuth (such as account identifiers and publishing/upload tokens) needed to upload or manage videos on authorized accounts, along with local media files and review status logs processed for the workflow.

2. How Information Is Used

Authorized credentials and workflow data are used solely to authenticate requests, upload or queue approved video clips, and track operational status. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Configuration files, tokens, and video files are stored according to the deployment environment. Keep credentials and access tokens confidential and revoke access anytime from your TikTok account settings.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy or data access, reach out through the project repository or contact channel associated with this application.
